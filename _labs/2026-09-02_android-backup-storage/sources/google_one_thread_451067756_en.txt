Android device backup storage update & controls
Announcement
Hey everyone,
 
We want to share an update on how your Android device backup works with your Google Account storage. Currently, data from MMS and some app data backed up from your Android device count toward your Google Account storage. In the coming weeks, we’re updating our policy so that all data backed up from your Android device counts toward your Google Account storage quota. 
 
This helps create a more consistent experience and includes data types like:
SMS and MMS messages
Call history
Device settings
App data
This update already applies to new Android backup users and rolls out to existing users in the coming weeks. 
 
With this change, we're also introducing new controls on Android 9 and up. These controls allow you to select which data types and apps you want to back up, which gives you more control over your Android backup experience.
 
To manage your storage space and clear unnecessary files, we recommend you review which items take up space in your storage.
